Why this hour

Of every station-hour in the three days of this storm, none was heavier than this one. Kaiholena (0231), up the slope in Kaʻū, took 4.37 inches in the sixty minutes to 20:30 HST — more than half again the next-heaviest gauge anywhere on the island that hour, Kona Hema at 2.64 in/hr. The radar has 2.67 in/hr falling on Nāʻālehu itself, 11 km down the slope from Kaiholena.

That is the hour Nāʻālehu flooded, and the reason is not in any daily table: flooding follows the rate, not the total. Kaiholena's 24.41 in for the day ranks only fourth on the island. The island's wettest gauge for the day was Laupāhoehoe with 32.12 in — a third more rain — but it was taking it at 1.95 in/hr during this same hour, hard rain that the ground and the streams could still carry. Rain that arrives in an hour has nowhere to go.
