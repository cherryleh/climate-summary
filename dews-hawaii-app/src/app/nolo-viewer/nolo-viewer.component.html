Tropical Storm Nolo Viewer

This website is designed to enable access to the most recent rainfall and wind speed data from Hawaiʻi Mesonet stations. Those stations record and transmit data every 5 minutes for numerous different variables. With the approach of the tropical storm, interest is mainly on rainfall and wind speed. Here you can find up-to-date rainfall accumulation and maximum wind and wind gust speeds for the period starting September 22, 10:00 PM HST. Data will be updated every 15 minutes.
